Our Story

A LEGACY OF
EXCELLENCE

Since Ferdinand Porsche founded the company in 1948, we have relentlessly pursued one goal: to build the world's most purposeful sports cars. Every model carries the weight of that ambition - and the joy of surpassing it.

1948 First Porsche 356 rolls out of a makeshift workshop in Gmünd, Austria.
1963 The 911 debuts at the Frankfurt Motor Show - an icon is born.
1973 The 911 Carrera RS 2.7 sets the benchmark for lightweight sports cars.
2019 The Taycan launches - Porsche's bold leap into an electric future.
